Project Overview

Cores & Scrap Roguelite is my ongoing solo game development project soon publish on Play store. It is an isometric shooter built around vehicle-based combat, room encounters, resource collection, and deep progression. The game features a lowpoly industrial visual style with machinery, platforms, and atmospheric lighting effects.

My Role & Gameplay Systems

As the sole developer, my focus has been on designing and implementing interconnected gameplay systems that work together seamlessly. Key implemented features include:

  • Vehicle Mechanics: Custom movement, health, and drifting physics for an isometric perspective.
  • Combat & Weapons: Modular turret/weapon systems featuring Assault, LMG, SMG, Shotgun, and Sniper variants.
  • Progression & Economy: A dual-currency system (Scrap and Cores) driving vehicle selection, garage progression, and weapon upgrades.
  • Level Design: Room-based enemy encounters and zone-based level progression.
  • Platform Specifics: Dedicated mobile control options and UI scaling for Android devices.

Technical Challenges

The primary challenge of this project has been managing the complexity of interconnected systems. Unlike isolated mechanics, a roguelite requires the economy, upgrade system, enemy AI, and vehicle physics to communicate flawlessly. I approached this by building modular C# clear clean scripts without depend on script to other, allowing me to iterate on weapon balance or enemy behavior without breaking the core progression loop.

Development Results & Next Steps

The project successfully reached a milestone with a playable demo published on itch.io in September 2026. This demo validates the core gameplay loop and mobile controls. Current development is focused on expanding the weapon roster, refining enemy encounter variety, and further optimizing the URP pipeline for a wider range of Android devices.
