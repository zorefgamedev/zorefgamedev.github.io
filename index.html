About me & 01 Theory

I am Feroz Haider, a game developer who enjoys building complete game systems from the ground up. My work combines gameplay programming, level design, technical problem-solving, and performance optimization. I work independently, handling everything from implementing mechanics to testing builds and preparing games for release.

Online, I share my development journey under the identity Zoref. My projects are developed and published under 01 Theory, my independent game development studio identity. While currently a solo operation, 01 Theory represents my commitment to building a consistent, professional presence and my long-term ambition to grow it into a recognized studio.

My development philosophy is simple: learn by building real projects, solve practical technical challenges, and prioritize completing playable experiences over endless ideation.
